How to Play

The Fugitive is trying to reach an Exit station without being caught. The Enterprise Crew is trying to catch them first. Either side can be one person, or more than one person sharing a device and deciding together -- that's up to you.

Each round: the Fugitive secretly moves first, then the Crew moves. The Crew can't see where the Fugitive actually is -- only what kind of route they used to get there, or nothing at all if the Fugitive used a Black ticket (or didn't move) to stay hidden.

Moving: tap a highlighted station to go there.

Reaching an Exit lets the Fugitive choose to end the game right there -- gold is worth the most, bronze the least, but any Exit is a win for the Fugitive. Passing through one doesn't end the game unless the Fugitive says so.
